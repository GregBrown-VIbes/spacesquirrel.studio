Install Kneady Controls

  1. In Blender, open Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions.
  2. Search for Kneady Controls and click Install.
  3. In the 3D viewport, press N and open the Kneady tab.

Kneady Controls needs Blender 4.2 or later. Updates arrive through the same Extensions page.

Install Kneady Controls Pro

  1. Download the zip from your Superhive library.
  2. In Blender, open Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions, click the menu at the top right, and choose Install from Disk.
  3. Pick the zip. Pro sits alongside the free Kneady Controls and adds its own panel at the top of the Kneady tab.

Getting help fast

Post in #kneady-help on the Discord with:

  • Your Blender version and Kneady version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • A screenshot or short clip
  • For errors: Window → Toggle System Console, then paste the red text

Pro owners: message your order in the Discord to get the Pro role and access to the beta channel.
